🏛️ Flagship Product

Muselry

나만의 문화지도

Muselry is a cultural lifestyle platform for iOS and Android. It helps users explore thousands of museums, art galleries, science centers, and cultural venues across Korea — and build a personal map of every place they've visited or want to visit.

Whether you're a seasoned culture enthusiast or just starting to explore, Muselry makes every outing more meaningful and every memory worth keeping.

Get it on Google Play
🏛️
Features

Everything you need
to explore culture

🗺️

Cultural Space Discovery

Browse thousands of museums, art galleries, science centers, and cultural venues across Korea. Filter by category, region, and rating.

📍

Map-Based Search

Find cultural spaces near you with an intuitive map interface. See what's around you and plan your next cultural outing with ease.

📖

Visit Tracking

Log every visit and build a personal history of your cultural experiences. Your own cultural map, growing with every trip.

⭐

Reviews & Ratings

Share your thoughts and read reviews from other culture enthusiasts. Help the community discover the best experiences.

🔖

Bookmarks & Wishlist

Save places you want to visit and organize your cultural wishlist. Plan future visits with your personal collection.

🏆

Culture Level System

Earn points and level up as you explore more cultural spaces. Gamified exploration makes every visit rewarding.

👶

Child-Friendly Recommendations

Find cultural spaces with facilities and programs suitable for children. Perfect for families seeking educational outings.

🤖

AI-Powered Suggestions

Smart recommendations based on your visit history, preferences, and location — so you always discover something new.
