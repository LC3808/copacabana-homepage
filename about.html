About Us

We connect culture

Copacabana, Co. is a Seoul-based software company building mobile applications and AI services that make cultural experiences more accessible for everyone.

Company

Who we are

Copacabana, Co. (주식회사 코파카바나) is a technology company headquartered in Seoul, South Korea. We specialize in mobile application development and AI-powered services, with a focus on cultural discovery and lifestyle platforms.

Founded with the belief that technology can make culture more accessible, we are building products that connect people with the rich cultural heritage of Korea. Our flagship product, Muselry, helps users explore museums, art galleries, and science centers across the country — turning every cultural outing into a lasting, shareable memory.
